Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

GTA6 Clipper is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses the YouTube Data API, TikTok and Instagram APIs solely to upload videos to, and read view statistics from, the owner's own accounts.

It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google, TikTok or Meta account settings.

Uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.
